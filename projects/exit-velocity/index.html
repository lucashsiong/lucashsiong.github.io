Exit velocity vs. launch angle

Pick a team or a player and see which batted balls turned into hits and home runs. The data covers every hitter in the 2025 regular season.

Loading data…

Data: Baseball Savant / MLB Statcast, pulled with pybaseball. Hover over a dot for details. Teams reflect who the batter played for in each game.

The question

I put this graph together because I was curious about the batted ball profiles for each type of hit. Were there any launch angles or exit velocities that were particularly associated with certain outcomes?

The data

I pulled pitch-level Statcast data for the whole 2025 regular season with the pybaseball package, then kept the balls put in play that had a measured exit velocity and launch angle. Plate appearances are counted from the pitches that ended each one, so they can be off by a few. The dotted line marks 95 mph, which Statcast calls a hard-hit ball. For speed, the chart shows a random sample of 6,000 dots when more balls are selected, but the stats above it use every ball.

What I found

Home runs by far had the tightest cluster of launch angles and exit velocities - which makes sense since the ball needs to be hit at a high angle and with power to clear the wall. For singles and doubles, it seems like there's more variability in their profiles, but still a sweet spot where they tend to cluster. Triples showed a more dispersed pattern, with almost all of them being hard hit but all with varying launch angles.
